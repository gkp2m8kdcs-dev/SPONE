Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about privacy, data, and how SPONE works.

Does SPONE connect to my brokerage?

No. SPONE never requests account credentials, never connects to a brokerage, and never has access to your real trading accounts. It is a self-contained tracking and journaling tool.

Where is my data stored?

Everything — portfolios, trades, journal entries, and attached photos — is stored locally on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Are the prices real-time?

SPONE is an educational tracking tool. Prices, charts, news and dividend events shown in the app are generated locally for demonstration purposes and do not represent live, tradable market data.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription. No in-app purchases. No ads. SPONE is a one-time purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Data → Reset All Data, or simply delete the app. All local data is permanently removed.
