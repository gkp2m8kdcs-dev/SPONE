1. Summary in plain English

SPONE is built around a single principle: your investment data is yours, and it stays on your device. We do not run a backend that stores your portfolios, trades, journal entries, or photos. We do not have a user database. There is no account to create, no email to verify, and no server-side profile tied to you.

The short version:

  • We collect nothing about you, your holdings, or how you use SPONE beyond what is required by Apple to deliver the App Store.
  • Everything you create lives locally on your iPhone inside the app's secure sandbox.
  • We never sell data. We have no data to sell, because we never collected any.
  • You can wipe everything by deleting the app or using the in-app "Reset All Data" option.

2. Information we collect

SPONE itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The app is fully functional offline.

The only data SPONE handles is data you enter directly into the app while it is running on your device:

  • Portfolio names, colors, starting cash and strategy notes you create.
  • Trade records (symbol, quantity, price, fees, date, thesis note).
  • Watchlist entries.
  • Journal entries and dividend events.
  • Photos you choose to attach from your photo library or capture with the camera.

All of the above is stored inside the iOS app sandbox and is accessible only to SPONE on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Information we do not collect

To be explicit, SPONE does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information.
  • Location data, contacts, calendar events, or health data.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA), device fingerprints, or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • Analytics events, crash logs tied to a user profile, or in-app behavioral telemetry.
  • Biometric data, payment data, or brokerage credentials.

4. Device permissions we request

SPONE requests only the minimum permissions required to function. You can decline any of these and the app will continue to work — only the relevant feature will be unavailable.

  • Photo Library (read): so you can import chart screenshots and reference images into a trade note or portfolio photo album. We only access photos you explicitly select.
  • Photo Library (write): so you can save annotated charts and screenshots back to your photo library when you choose to export them.
  • Camera: so you can capture photos of paper notes, broker statements or receipts and attach them directly to a trade.

SPONE does not request microphone access, contacts, calendars, location services, motion data, or background tracking.

5. Third-party services

SPONE does not embed analytics SDKs, advertising networks, social login providers, crash reporters, or any third-party tracking code. Apple's standard App Store infrastructure (used only when you install or update the app through the App Store) is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's privacy

SPONE is designed for adult investors. The app is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, the app is structurally compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent regulations worldwide.

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy — for example, to add a clearly optional cloud sync feature in the future — we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and surface a prominent notice inside the app before any change takes effect. Because we do not have your contact information, we cannot email you about updates; please revisit this page periodically.
