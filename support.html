Frequently asked questions

How do I get in touch with support?

Email Mefepzavap@icloud.com. We typically respond within 2 business days. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

The app won't open / crashes immediately
  • Make sure your iPhone runs iOS 16 or later (Settings → General → About → iOS Version).
  • Force-quit the app and re-open it (swipe up from the bottom, then swipe SPONE away).
  • Restart your iPhone.
  • Reinstall the app: delete SPONE, then redownload it from the App Store. Your local data will be lost — there is no cloud backup.

If the issue persists, please email support with the steps that reproduce it.

I lost my portfolios after updating the app

SPONE stores data inside the iOS app sandbox. If you uninstall the app or restore your iPhone from a backup that predates the install, the local data will not be available. We do not have a copy and cannot restore it on your behalf. Going forward, please avoid deleting the app and consider keeping regular iCloud device backups enabled.

Are the prices in the app real?

No. SPONE is an educational tracking tool. Prices, charts, news and dividend events shown in the app are generated locally for demonstration. Please do not use SPONE to place trades or as a source of live market data. See Terms of Use, section 3.

Can I sync my data between iPhone and iPad?

Not currently. SPONE is single-device by design — every portfolio, trade and journal entry lives in the local app sandbox. Multi-device sync is a frequently requested feature and is on our roadmap.

How do I delete all my data?

Open the app → Settings → Data → Reset All Data. Or simply delete the app from your home screen. Both options permanently erase everything stored on your device.

Does SPONE support Apple Watch, Mac, or Android?

SPONE is currently iPhone-only, built for iOS 16 and up. We are evaluating other platforms based on user demand.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Open the App Store → tap your profile icon → tap your name → tap "Purchase History" → find SPONE → tap "Report a Problem" → choose "Request a refund". The developer cannot issue refunds directly.

I'm a journalist / creator — who do I contact for press?

Please email Mefepzavap@icloud.com with the subject line "Press". We'll do our best to respond quickly.
